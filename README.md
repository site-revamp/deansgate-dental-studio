# Deansgate Dental Studio – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Deansgate Dental Studio is a premium private dental practice located at Deansgate Square in Manchester, United Kingdom. The practice provides general, cosmetic, restorative, orthodontic, implant and advanced dental treatments.

The redesigned homepage positions the practice as a premium, patient-focused dental destination, combining advanced clinical services with a boutique practice experience. It targets individuals and families seeking private dental care, cosmetic improvements and specialist treatments in Manchester.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Dental Service Discovery

**Previous issue:** The wide range of dental treatments needed clearer organisation to help visitors identify relevant services.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Structured dental services into clear treatment categories.
- Improved the presentation of cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign, dental implants and general dentistry.
- Created a clearer content hierarchy for exploring treatment options.

**Result:** Visitors can identify relevant dental services more easily and navigate towards specific treatments.

### 2. Stronger Premium Brand Presentation

**Previous issue:** The practice's premium positioning, clinical expertise and distinctive patient experience needed stronger visual communication.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Refined the homepage's visual direction to reflect the premium dental brand.
- Improved the presentation of the clinical team and advanced dental capabilities.
- Created clearer sections highlighting personalised care and the practice environment.
- Improved the use and positioning of dental imagery.

**Result:** The homepage communicates the practice's premium positioning, professional capabilities and patient-focused approach more effectively.

### 3. Clearer Appointment Conversion Pathways

**Previous issue:** Important commercial actions needed stronger visibility to help visitors progress from exploring treatments to arranging appointments.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Improved the placement of appointment booking calls to action.
- Strengthened pathways towards cosmetic, Invisalign and implant enquiries.
- Made essential contact information easier to locate.
- Improved the presentation of online booking and contact options.

**Result:** Visitors have clearer routes to enquire about treatments and arrange private dental appointments.

## 3. Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage includes the following visual and usability refinements:

- **Visual identity:** Established a more consistent premium dental aesthetic reflecting the practice's boutique positioning.
- **Layout:** Improved section organisation, spacing and content hierarchy.
- **Typography:** Refined heading and body text presentation for better readability.
- **Imagery:** Improved the integration of dental and practice-related visuals.
- **Content consistency:** Created a more structured presentation of services, clinical expertise and patient information.
- **Navigation:** Improved access to important treatment categories and appointment information.
- **User experience:** Created a clearer journey from discovering dental services to exploring treatments and booking appointments.
- **Responsive presentation:** Adapted the homepage layout for desktop and mobile browsing.

## 4. Final Outcome

The redesigned Deansgate Dental Studio homepage delivers a more structured presentation of the practice's services, clinical capabilities and premium patient experience.

The three main improvements focus on dental service discovery, premium brand communication and appointment conversion.

The resulting design provides visitors with a clearer understanding of the practice and more accessible pathways towards booking private dental care.
